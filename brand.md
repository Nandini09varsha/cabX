# Brand — CabX

_Status: applied_

CabX is a decentralized ride-hailing product. The palette follows a 60 / 30 / 10 rule: warm parchment surfaces, espresso ink structure, and a single harvest-gold accent (taxi heritage without neon yellow).

## Palette

| Token | Light | Dark | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| background | `#F3EEE4` | `#14110D` | 60% canvas |
| foreground | `#1C1914` | `#F3EDE3` | body text |
| card | `#FFFDF8` | `#1C1814` | elevated surfaces |
| muted-foreground | `#5F574C` | `#B8AFA2` | secondary text (AA) |
| primary | `#C47B0A` | `#E4B84A` | 10% accent |
| ink | `#1C1914` | `#1C1814` | 30% structure / CTAs |

Do not mix cool gray with this warm ramp. Do not use pure black or neon taxi yellow as large fills.
